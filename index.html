01 — Case Study

SightMate

An accessibility-first navigation app for the visually impaired.

ContextGraduation Project
RoleUI/UX Designer
ToolFigma
TypeMobile App

The brief

Design a mobile app that helps visually impaired individuals navigate their environment safely and independently — reducing reliance on others for everyday movement.

Key features

  • AI camera assistance — real-time scene description via the phone's camera.
  • Voice interaction — audio-first navigation and command system.
  • GPS navigation — outdoor guidance with clear audio cues.
  • Companion tracking — a trusted contact can follow the journey (with mutual consent).
  • Emergency triple-tap — a discreet gesture to instantly alert a contact.

Design focus

Accessibility came first — every decision was filtered through audio-first interaction, high-contrast visuals, and generous touch targets. The challenge was making a complex system feel calm and trustworthy.

02 — Case Study

Abla Fadila

A children's storytelling app preserving traditional Arabic stories.

ContextCollaboration — Fine Arts
RoleUI/UX Designer
ToolFigma
TypeMobile App

The brief

The original project was a graduation project by a Fine Arts student celebrating the legacy of Abla Fadila — a beloved figure in traditional Arabic storytelling. My role was to design the digital experience: a mobile app for children to discover these stories.

What I designed

  • A warm, child-friendly interface with large touch targets and clear navigation.
  • Illustrated story cards that mirror the visual language of traditional books.
  • Audio narration and read-along moments for early readers.
  • A gentle visual hierarchy that puts the story — not the app — at the center.

Design focus

Cultural preservation through thoughtful design. The interface needed to feel familiar to children, respectful to the source material, and easy for parents to navigate alongside their kids.

03 — Case Study

Power Coach

An online fitness coaching platform for trainers and clients.

ContextUniversity Assignment
RoleUI/UX Designer
ToolFigma
TypeMobile App

The brief

Design a fitness coaching app that connects users with personal trainers — covering workout programs, subscriptions, and guided exercise flows in one interface.

Key features

  • Structured workout programs with clear progression.
  • Online personal trainer subscriptions with tiered pricing.
  • Step-by-step exercise instructions with visuals.
  • Subscription management in a clear, low-friction flow.

Design focus

Motivation-driven design. The interface had to feel energetic without becoming noisy — using strong typography, clear hierarchy, and progress signals to keep users engaged over time.
